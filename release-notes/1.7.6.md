## What's changed in 1.7.6

**Fix: titles that briefly drop out of your media server no longer lose their posters.**

Media servers sometimes stop listing titles for a while: a drive or network share goes offline, or a library is being
re-scanned. Until now, the next Postarr scan removed those titles straight away, with your chosen posters and the
backups of your original artwork. When the server listed them again, often under new IDs, Postarr treated them as
brand-new titles and picked fresh posters automatically.

Now:

- **A title the server stops listing is hidden, not removed.** It disappears from the grids, but Postarr keeps it for
  **7 days**. If it comes back in that time (with the same ID, or a new one thanks to the re-add matching in 1.7.3), it
  picks up exactly where it left off: your poster, background, season posters, backups and added date.
- **After 7 days, a few missing titles are removed automatically.** A large batch (more than 25, or 5% of the library)
  is never removed on its own. That looks far more like a fault than hundreds of deliberate deletions, so it waits for
  you.
- **New on the Health page: "titles no longer in Plex"** (or Jellyfin / Emby). It shows how many titles are hidden,
  since when, and a few of their names, with a **Remove now** button if they really are gone.

**Tip for Plex users:** turn off *Settings → Library → Empty trash automatically after every scan* in Plex. Then a
drive that drops out leaves its titles marked unavailable, and they keep their IDs when it comes back.
