## What's changed in 1.7.4

**Fix: scans no longer replace posters you've chosen.**

When a movie's quality changed (resolution, HDR or Dolby Vision), Postarr picked and applied a fresh poster, replacing
the one you'd chosen. Worse, a busy Plex server could make this happen to the same movies on every scan. That most
often happened overnight, when Plex runs its own maintenance at the same time as Postarr's scheduled scans.

- **A quality change now keeps your poster.** Only the badges change: the same poster is re-rendered with the new
  badges and pushed again (straight away with *Auto-apply on scan*, otherwise via **Apply All**). Items with no
  poster yet still get an automatic pick. The Activity log says *"Quality changed: … keeping its poster"*.
- **A slow or busy Plex can't fake a quality change any more.** Postarr reads HDR / Dolby Vision from each movie's
  details in batches. A batch that timed out used to be skipped silently, so those movies looked "SDR" until the next
  scan and flipped back again. Failed batches are now retried in smaller pieces, and anything still unavailable keeps
  its known HDR / Dolby Vision instead of being treated as changed.

If posters were replaced before this update, open the movie and pick your poster again. It will stay put from now on.
