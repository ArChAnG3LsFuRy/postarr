## What's changed in 1.7.10

**Fix: Health now really takes you to the title.**

In 1.7.9, clicking a title in a Health list opened the right grid and highlighted the card, but in a large library the
card could end up just out of view, below the visible area of a 1,600-poster grid. Postarr now jumps straight to the
card's exact position and checks it again once the posters around it have loaded, so the highlighted card is always
in the middle of the screen.

**Fix: no stale page after an update.**

Browsers could keep showing the previous version's page for a while after you installed an update, so new features
seemed to be missing until you pressed Ctrl+F5. Postarr's pages are now always re-checked, while images, scripts and
styles are still cached.
