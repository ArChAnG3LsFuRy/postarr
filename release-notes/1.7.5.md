## What's changed in 1.7.5

**New: "Recently added" and "Recently changed" on the Movie and Show poster pages.**

The filter dropdown at the top of **Movie Posters** and **Show Posters** has two new choices. Each shows the newest
100 titles, newest first:

- **Recently added:** titles your media server added most recently. For shows, a newly arrived season counts too.
- **Recently changed:** titles whose poster Postarr pushed to your server most recently. For shows, season posters
  count too. Handy for checking what a scan or Apply All just did.

The A–Z bar hides while one of these is selected (the list is in date order) and comes back when you choose another
filter.
