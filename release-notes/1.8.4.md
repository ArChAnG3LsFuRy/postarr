## What's changed in 1.8.4

**Fix: "In use" was on the wrong poster in Your previous posters.**

After picking a new poster, opening the picker again could still mark the *old* poster as **In use**, because the
picker went by its own copy of the title, which hadn't caught up yet (always the case for season posters). The
poster in use is now worked out by Postarr itself each time the picker opens, so the tag is always on the right one.
