## What's changed in 1.8.3

**New: poster history, so you can go back to an earlier poster.**

Postarr now remembers the artwork you've used. When you open the picker for a movie, show, season or background,
a **Your previous posters** row at the top shows the ones used before, newest first. The one in use now is marked
**In use**. Click any earlier one to put it back.

- Every poster that's sent to your media server counts: ones you picked, ones a scan applied automatically, and your
  own uploads.
- The first time a poster changes after updating, the poster it replaces is remembered too, so you can undo that
  first change straight away.
- The last 12 are kept for each poster, background and season poster.
- **Restore original** still goes back to the artwork your media server had before Postarr changed it.
