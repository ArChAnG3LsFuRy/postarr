## What's changed in 1.7.1

**Fix: other PCs on your network couldn't open Postarr (Windows installer).**

In 1.7.0 the installer only opened Postarr's port (5286) on networks Windows calls *Private* or *Domain*. Windows
labels many home networks *Public*, though, and on those every other device was blocked: `http://<server-ip>:5286`
just timed out, while `http://localhost:5286` on the server itself still worked.

The firewall rule now works on every network type but **only accepts connections from your local network**
(Windows' "LocalSubnet"). Anything outside the network the PC is on is still blocked, and Postarr's own login
protects it within your network. Installing 1.7.1 over 1.7.0 replaces the old rule automatically.

- Nothing else changes, and your settings, database and artwork backups are kept.
- Docker is unaffected (it doesn't use the Windows firewall); the 1.7.1 image is otherwise identical to 1.7.0.
- Reaching Postarr from a *different* subnet (for example another VLAN, or over a VPN) needs your own firewall rule.
  Remote access through a reverse proxy or tunnel running on the same PC keeps working.
