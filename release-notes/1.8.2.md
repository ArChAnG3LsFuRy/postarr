## What's changed in 1.8.2

**New: open a poster's source page from the picker.**

Every choice in the poster and background picker now has a small **↗** in its bottom-right corner. It opens the
page the image comes from in a new tab, so you can see the rest of that artist's work or check a set:

- **TMDB:** the title's posters page (or backgrounds page, or that season's posters for a season poster)
- **FanArt.tv:** the movie's or show's page, with every poster and set by every artist
- **TheTVDB:** the movie's or show's page

Clicking the poster itself still picks it, as before. Images from your media server, your own uploads and
collection posters drawn by Postarr have no source page, so they have no ↗.
