## What's changed in 1.7.2

**New: clean up libraries that no longer exist on your media server (Health page).**

If a library is deleted and re-created (for example while recovering Plex from a backup), Plex gives the
temporary library and its items new IDs. Postarr adds those items, and once the original library is restored
they're left behind as **duplicate shows or movies**, often with blank posters. Scans deliberately never remove
them on their own: if a library vanishes for a while (a restore in progress, a server still starting up),
automatically removing it would wipe your chosen posters and the backups of your original artwork.

Postarr now spots these and lists them on the **Health** page under *"Libraries no longer in Plex"* (or
Jellyfin / Emby), with the number of items and a few titles. Click **Remove from Postarr** and confirm to clear
them out.

- Only Postarr's own records for that library are removed (items, their chosen posters and stored
  original-poster backups). Nothing on your media server changes, and your current libraries aren't touched.
- The library is checked again against your server when you click Remove. If it's back, nothing is removed.
- After each scan, the Activity log mentions any such libraries.
