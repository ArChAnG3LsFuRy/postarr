## What's changed in 1.8.1

**New: Quick scan.**

Under **Scan Library** there's now a **Quick scan** button. It checks only new and changed titles, the same as the
scheduled incremental scans, so it finishes in seconds instead of minutes. Use it after adding something to your
library when you don't want to wait for the next scheduled scan.

- **Scan Library** is still a full scan: it re-checks every title, its ratings and its artwork.
- Until Postarr has completed one full scan, Quick scan runs a full scan (it needs that first one as a baseline).
