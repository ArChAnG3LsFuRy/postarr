## What's changed in 1.7.9

**New: fixing a match in Plex now finishes the job in Postarr.**

Sometimes a title reaches Postarr without proper IDs, because Plex hasn't matched it or has matched it to the wrong
thing. That can happen after restoring Plex's database from an older backup. Postarr can't find posters for it, and
can't tell it's a title it already knew, so its earlier record shows as "no longer in Plex".

Now, as soon as Plex has the right match (**⋯ → Fix Match…** in Plex) and Postarr scans:

- **If Postarr still has the title's earlier record** (hidden as missing), it recognises it and brings back your chosen
  poster, background, season posters and original-artwork backups, then removes the old record. No duplicate is left.
- **Otherwise, if the title still has no poster,** Postarr picks one automatically, the same way as for a new title.
- A poster you've already chosen is never replaced.

**Improved: Health jumps straight to the title.**

Clicking a title in any Health list (*No poster*, *Not applied*, *Missing ratings*, …) now opens its grid, clears
any search or filter that would hide it, scrolls to its card and highlights it. Before, it only opened the grid.
